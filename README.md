## The joke

> My barber charges $40 for the cut and $0 for the advice — last Tuesday he told me to leave my wife and keep the dog.

## Why it wins

Against the judge's five criteria, in order:

1. **A real one-line joke** — clear setup ($40 cut, $0 advice) and a payoff in the final clause.
2. **Laughs out loud, not just a smile** — the advice escalates from a freebie to a life-ruining verdict in one breath.
3. **Turns on a surprise** — a barber's tip is expected to be about hair; instead it is about leaving your wife and keeping the dog.
4. **Fresh angle** — a named person, a priced service and a named weekday; no smart device, no "I asked my X", no restraining order, none of the field's modal angles.
5. **Wastes no words** — every word earns its place: $40, $0, last Tuesday, wife, dog. Two beats, 116 characters, nothing to trim.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node, no dependencies, no network, no randomness) writes the line above plus a trailing newline to `/output/entry.txt`.

Local check: the program was run with `OUTPUT_DIR` pointed at a scratch folder and the produced `entry.txt` was verified byte-identical to the joke above plus a newline — 116 characters, inside the 1–200 gate.
